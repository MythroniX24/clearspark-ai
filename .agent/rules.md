## 🔒 Mandatory Training Directive: 100% Real Photography Only

1. **PROHIBITION OF SYNTHETIC TEXTURES**:
   - The AI model MUST NEVER be trained on procedurally generated synthetic textures, sine-wave noises, or mathematical noise patches.
   - All production and research training datasets MUST consist strictly of **100% authentic, real-world high-resolution photography** (e.g., MS-COCO 2017, Places365, Unsplash, Flickr, DIV2K).

2. **COMPOSITION OF THE 20,000+ REAL IMAGE DATASET**:
   - **Dark & Night Real Photography**: Real night-sky captures, real deep shadows, real indoor low-light photos.
   - **High-Frequency Real Textures**: Real human skin/portraits, real architectural walls, real foliage/nature, real clothing/textiles.
   - **Real Landscapes & Horizons**: Real sky gradients, sunsets, real lens bokeh.

3. **AUTOMATIC REPOSITORY PIPELINE**:
   - All training pipelines and Jupyter Notebooks must directly fetch and load from real image repositories (e.g. Kaggle COCO 2017 / Unsplash public datasets) without falling back to synthetic patch generation.
